Urban Tram Planner Alpha 0.6

Controlled Transport Fever 3 modding test for automatic tram-track conversion on an existing radial street corridor.

v0.6 fixes the v0.5 detection bug (street tram lanes use TRAM/ELECTRIC_TRAM, not rail TRAM_TRACK), filters STREET templates, preserves car access, excludes bridges/tunnels, and re-validates every segment sequentially before building. No direct mutation of engine BaseEdge lane configs.
